**Study:** Training an on-device computer program that helps people drape a saree.
**Run by:** [name, institution, contact phone/email]. **Ethics approval:** [IEC name, reference no.]

## What is this study?
We are building an app that watches someone drape a saree through a camera and gives guidance.
To teach it, we need video of real people draping sarees, including some deliberate mistakes.

## What will I do?
One session of about 75–90 minutes in a private room at [place]:
* You change into a blouse and petticoat **in private, off camera**. Recording starts only when
  you say you are ready.
* Two cameras record you while you drape two sarees, twice each (four drapes in total). In two
  of the drapes we will ask you to make small, deliberate mistakes, such as uneven pleats, and
  then fix them.
* A 2-minute clip of simple movements (turning, raising your arms).
* You can take breaks, pause, or stop at any time.

## Who will see the recordings?
Only the named research team ([names/roles]) and trained annotators, who see still frames
rather than whole videos. Recordings are stored on an encrypted drive that is never uploaded to
the internet or any cloud service. Your name is kept separately from the recordings; the data is
labelled with a code (for example P012).

## Will my face be shown?
Your face is recorded, because the program needs to see your whole body. It will **never** be
shown publicly unless you tick the box allowing that, and even then it will be blurred.

## Can I withdraw?
Yes, at any time, without giving a reason, until **[date of first dataset release]**. Contact
[contact]. We will delete your recordings and remove you from future versions of the program's
training data. After that date, models already trained cannot be "untrained", but your data
will not be used in any new version.

## Risks and benefits
The main risk is discomfort at being filmed while dressing. You control when recording
starts, and you can stop at any time. You receive [compensation] even if you stop early. There
is no direct benefit to you beyond that.

## Who can take part?
Adults aged 18 or over. No experience with sarees is needed; beginners are especially welcome.

## How long is data kept?
Raw video: until [date or "N months after annotation"]. Annotated still frames: until
[date], and only if you agree in the consent form.

Questions or complaints: [researcher contact]; independent contact: [ethics committee contact].
